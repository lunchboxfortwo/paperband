Strategy

Once each month-end, each tracked name is set long, short, or flat from the sign of its prior 252-session adjusted return. Those names share a gross cap of twice paper equity. The book checks exposure every five minutes, and a 10% drop from that session's starting equity flattens it for the rest of the session. Any fill is the next regular-session 9:30 a.m. Eastern open.
